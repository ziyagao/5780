Week9: Digital Senses

COMM5780M Digital Practices

This workshop included practical electronics work and discussion about technology. I made simple switches and was interested in the idea “being is more important than knowing”. I used to only focus on learning technical skills, and rarely think about the ethical problems of the tools I make.


The examples of biased data from wearable devices and attendance surveillance systems made me think a lot. Technology is not neutral. Even if a data system works well, it may create unfair power and hidden harm.


This changes how I do my digital media projects. Technical skills are useful, but I will think about ethics early in my design. I will think about who my work influences and what possible risks it may bring.
