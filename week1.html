Week 1: Introducing Digital Practices

Week 1 introduced core theoretical stances for analysing digital media. I noticed how mainstream media coverage of generative AI often falls into technological determinism, framing technology as an independent force that directly changes human cognition. This oversimplified viewpoint ignores social factors such as investor goals, policy and user agency.

While SCOT highlights social forces shaping technological success or failure, the module favours social‑shaping theory, which emphasises mutual interaction between society and technology. Google Glass’s commercial failure illustrates this point: social worries over privacy, not merely technical flaws, led to its decline.

Additionally, I learnt digital technologies embed designers’ assumptions and biases; their impacts are not unavoidable. The session also clarified ground‑rules for generative AI use in study. Moving forward, I aim to avoid purely descriptive writing and practise critical thinking by questioning wider social implications behind digital‑technology debates.
