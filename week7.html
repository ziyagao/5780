Week 7: Reflective Pause

This week’s workshop centred on Sumpter’s social media experiment. Drawing on Cheney-Lippold and Safiya Umoja Noble’s work on algorithmic identity, our group recognised manual coding and consumer profiling create biased digital representations. The optional interview task highlighted how research medium shapes collected data, alongside ethical rules for anonymised notes and no audio recording.

I also noted the module AI policy: AI may only be used for proofreading and translation and must be acknowledged. Like platform algorithms and human coding choices, AI carries inherent bias. I will retain original authorship and critically evaluate AI tools when writing my essay and portfolio work.
